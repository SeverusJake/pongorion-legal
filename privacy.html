PongOrion Privacy Policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Developer: sutnes

In short: PongOrion does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. PongOrion does not collect any personal information or usage data, and it cannot send data anywhere: the app does not request internet access. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs, and it does not use device identifiers or location.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, PongOrion saves a small amount of information in the app's private storage on your device only:

  • your settings (volumes, screen shake, vibration, power-ups, orientation and match length);
  • your progress: unlocked opponents, the character you last picked and completed tutorials;
  • coins earned in the game and the power-ups they bought;
  • your best scores and times, and your achievements.

This information never leaves your device and is not linked to you. Uninstalling PongOrion, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it.

Purchases

PongOrion has no real-money purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought.

Permissions

PongOrion asks for one permission: vibration, used for short game feedback such as ball hits and goals. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.

Children

PongOrion is suitable for players of all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children, including children under 13. Parents and guardians can contact us with any questions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If PongOrion ever starts collecting any information, this policy will be updated before that version of the app is released.
