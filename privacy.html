PongOrion Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Developer: sutnes

In short: PongOrion is free and shows ads. We (the developer) do not collect personal information and have no accounts or servers. Our advertising partners receive device and ad data to show ads, and Google Play handles in-app purchases. Ads are non-personalised in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland; anywhere, you can change this in the game's Settings > Privacy. Your game progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves. PongOrion has no accounts, and the developer runs no servers. The game does contain two third-party services that process data on their own terms, described below: advertising and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

PongOrion shows ads: optional video ads you choose to watch for coins, and full-screen ads between some matches. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Mobile Ltd., part of Unity Technologies) and the ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these partners may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version;
  • your IP address and the coarse location it implies (country or region), and network type;
  • ad interaction data such as which ads were shown, watched to the end or tapped.

Personalised ads and your choice: if you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland (or the game cannot tell where you are), PongOrion shows only non-personalised ads and tells our partners that you have not consented to personalised advertising. Elsewhere, ads may be personalised using your advertising ID. Wherever you are, you can choose in Settings > Privacy: "Allow" turns on personalised ads, and "No thanks" turns them off and tells our partners that you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. The game determines your region from your mobile network, SIM card or phone region setting, on your device only. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

How these partners handle data is described in their own policies: Unity privacy policy (covers Unity Ads and LevelPlay).

Purchases

You can buy coin packs and "No Ads" with real money. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your payment details. Google shares with the app only what it needs to deliver the purchase, such as an order ID. See the Google privacy policy. Coins can still be earned for free by playing.

Information stored on your device

To work as a game, PongOrion saves a small amount of information in the app's private storage on your device only:

  • your settings (volumes, screen shake, vibration, power-ups, paddle spin, power-button side, orientation, match length and accessibility options);
  • your progress: unlocked opponents, the character you last picked, completed tutorials, Daily and Endless records, which story cards and tips you have seen;
  • coins earned or bought, and the power-ups and cosmetics they bought or that you equipped;
  • whether you own "No Ads", the order IDs of purchases already delivered (so a purchase is never delivered twice), your personalised-ads choice, and how many ads you have watched today.
  • your best scores and times, your achievements and your game stats (matches, wins, points, rallies and time played).

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling PongOrion, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes it. Purchases of "No Ads" are also remembered by Google Play and come back when you reinstall or tap Restore Purchases.

Permissions

PongOrion asks for: internet and network state (to load ads and connect to Google Play for purchases), advertising ID (used by the ad partners above), billing (Google Play purchases) and vibration (short game feedback such as ball hits and goals; you can turn it off in Settings).

Children

PongOrion is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly allow ad partners to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, before the version of the app it describes is released.
